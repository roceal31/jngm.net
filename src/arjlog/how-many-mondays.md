The checkbox on my habit tracker app says, "Write Something", an activity which I have not really been overly willing to tackle of late. It's so much easier to watch episodes of [Silicon Valley](https://youtu.be/8ZgfTarNxdY?si=ZjeCRgaxhxzg6Dp4) and play the remastered [Geneforge](https://www.spiderwebsoftware.com/geneforge/). I have entered full [countdown mode](/posts/there-is-a-season/).

Spring retreated slightly, and it's back to being chilly when the sun goes down. I have had to officially embrace middle age this month &mdash; my GP has informed me that my hormone levels are confirmed to be [doing that thing](https://www.jeanhailes.org.au/health-topics/menopause/) that most 40-something women's hormone levels do. This probably explains why I have been tired, depressed, have low motivation, and feel constantly overwhelmed by everything.

I feel incredibly boring having this be the only thing I can think of to write about.

Underneath all that bleah I have had these little bright spots of ideas. The [podcast](https://momentsofmadness.au/) is taking a brief hiatus, but my partner in crime and I have actually been cooking up a few things that at least we will enjoy. I've been spending lots of time discussing Big Ideas with the $Spouse. And the secret part of me who wants to write a novel for shits and giggles finally landed on a seed of an idea that I think I could expand, given time to tackle my writing atrophy.

Life keeps plugging on, Monday by Monday. Sixteen Mondays to go.
