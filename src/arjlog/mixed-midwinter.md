In an effort to at least *try* to make good on my goal of boosting my annual post count, I have finally shaken off the shackles of procrastination. Maybe it's the sunshine today, or the fact that we're significantly past the winter solstice, but I feel ready to tackle things. Of particular note was yesterday's mental health restoration exercise, in which I gave myself some psychological breathing room and got some lingering things off my to do list that were continually stressing me out.

I also fully indulged in a bit of dopamine-mining by digging into [Dredge](https://www.dredge.game/) and playing a solid few hours of a non-casual game. After finishing all of [Widow's Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Widow%27s_Bay) season 1, it felt tonally apt. I am enjoying both the creepiness and the open-world / explorative nature of the game. Plus, just cruising around on my little boat is definitely my kind of vibe.

I also finished [the latest Murderbot](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/240019874-platform-decay). I sparkily-heart-sigil you, Murderbot.

Another thing that has helped my brains out is that I was finally able to sit down with a new psychologist and carry on with [some of the unpacking](/posts/clearing-the-decks/) [I had been doing](/posts/burnout/) late last year. That has galvanised me to move ahead with new plans which I will probably post about later.

If you had asked me 2-3 weeks ago how I was handling things, I probably would have replied, "[abysmally](/posts/general-anxiety/)." It's funny how a day off and an hour's chat with the right person can turn that a complete 180º around.

Tomorrow we are gonna get our nerd on and go to [Winterfest](https://winterfest.com.au/). I really hope it will be fun, I've been intrigued to go for a while now. The kiddos seem keen, and it feels like there's something for everyone. We all just need a day out of our own personal ruts.

Next week kicks off the school holidays, which were always stressful when I was a pre-school and early school parent. These days it's nice to just avoid having to heard cats in the morning, and as almost-thirteen-year-olds, my kids are fairly decent at keeping themselves entertained, and we don't have to escort them everywhere.

Oh, and happy Fourth of July? I guess? I hope the US can collectively do some serious introspection on the current state of play and the significance of what a bunch of landed white dudes were trying to accomplish back in 1776, flawed as their perspective may seem these days. I don't have a lot of optimism that any great epiphanies will happen there, but it doesn't hurt to be hopeful.
